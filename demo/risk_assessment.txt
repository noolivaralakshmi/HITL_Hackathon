RISK ASSESSMENT
Project: Passkey Authentication Migration
Date: January 30, 2026
Author: Security & Risk Team
Risk ID: RA-2026-015

RISK REGISTER:

RISK-001: Device Compatibility Gap
Severity: MEDIUM
Likelihood: HIGH
Impact: 5% of users unable to use passkeys immediately
Mitigation: Implement security key fallback + cross-device QR authentication
Owner: DevOps Team
Status: Mitigation in progress

RISK-002: User Adoption Resistance
Severity: LOW
Likelihood: MEDIUM
Impact: Slower rollout timeline
Mitigation: Phased approach, training sessions, executive sponsorship
Owner: HR & Communications
Status: Communication plan drafted

RISK-003: Platform Authenticator Failure
Severity: MEDIUM
Likelihood: LOW
Impact: Users locked out if device biometric fails
Mitigation: Recovery codes + admin-assisted re-enrollment
Owner: IT Support
Status: Recovery flow designed

RISK-004: Vendor Lock-in
Severity: LOW
Likelihood: LOW
Impact: Dependency on Apple/Google/Microsoft authenticator ecosystems
Mitigation: Multi-platform passkey sync, portable credentials via FIDO2
Owner: Architecture Team
Status: Monitored

RISK-005: Regulatory Change
Severity: LOW
Likelihood: LOW
Impact: New regulations might mandate additional factors
Mitigation: Architecture supports layered authentication, can add factors
Owner: Compliance Team
Status: No current regulatory concerns

OVERALL RISK SCORE: MEDIUM
RECOMMENDATION: Proceed with migration. All identified risks have viable mitigations.

RISK ACCEPTANCE:
- CTO accepts residual risk of 5% device incompatibility
- CISO accepts transition period risk (dual auth systems for 6 months)
- VP Engineering accepts timeline risk (potential 2-week delay in Q2)

Signed: Risk Assessment Board
Date: January 31, 2026
