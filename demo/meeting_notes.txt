MEETING NOTES - Authentication Strategy Review
Date: January 15, 2026
Attendees: Sarah Chen (CISO), Marcus Johnson (VP Engineering), Dev Team Leads

AGENDA:
1. Current authentication vulnerabilities
2. Proposed migration to passkeys
3. Timeline and resource planning

DISCUSSION:

Sarah Chen opened the meeting by presenting Q4 2025 security metrics:
- 67% of security breaches involved compromised credentials
- 1,200 password reset tickets per month
- Average time to detect credential theft: 14 days
- Phishing simulation failure rate: 23% of employees

Marcus Johnson: "We need to move to a passwordless approach. Our current system is fundamentally insecure."

The team reviewed three options:
1. Keep passwords with enhanced MFA
2. Hardware MFA tokens (YubiKey)
3. Platform passkeys (WebAuthn/FIDO2)

After discussion, the team unanimously agreed on passkeys because:
- Phishing-resistant by design
- No hardware distribution needed
- Better user experience (biometric unlock)
- Industry standard (FIDO2/WebAuthn)

Hardware tokens were rejected due to:
- Cost: $45 per token × 10,000+ employees = $450K+ just for hardware
- Logistics: Distribution, replacement, inventory management
- User friction: Must carry physical token at all times

TIMELINE AGREED:
- Q1 2026: Pilot with IT department (1,000 users)
- Q2 2026: Full company rollout
- Q3 2026: Begin password deprecation for non-critical systems
